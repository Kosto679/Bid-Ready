Pixelridge

A software studio in Athens, Greece. We design and build web apps, booking platforms and data dashboards.

About us

Pixelridge was founded in 2019 in Athens. We are a team of 8 people: designers, developers and a project manager who work closely with our clients from the first workshop to years of support after launch. Most of our clients are in tourism and retail, and we increasingly work with the public sector.
