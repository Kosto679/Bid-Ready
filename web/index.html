2 · Ranked matches

Open Greek IT tenders, scored on TED data

Fit = 25% CPV overlap + 25% TED value vs your 3-year turnover + 25% place + 25% days to deadline (capped at 45). This is a shortlist score, not eligibility.

● 70–100 strong fit■ 40–69 partial fit▲ 0–39 weak fit
How is this scored?

CPV is the EU's Common Procurement Vocabulary: an 8-digit code for what a tender buys (72000000 = IT services: consulting, software development, Internet and support). We match on the first two digits, the division, against the codes guessed from your description. Score = share of the tender's codes in your divisions.

Value vs turnover = your 2023–2025 turnover ÷ (2 × the TED value), capped at 1. TED's value can include options, so this is rough. Place = 1 if the work is in Greece and so are you. Deadline = days left ÷ 45, capped at 1: more time to prepare scores higher.

Each factor counts 25%, so the score is 25 × the sum of the four, from 0 to 100.
